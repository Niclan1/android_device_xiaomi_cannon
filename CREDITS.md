# Credits and source provenance

This unofficial Redmi Note 9T (`cannong`, global variant) port builds on the work of the Android, Linux, LineageOS and MediaTek device communities. Thank you to the maintainers and original authors listed below. Original copyright notices, licenses, authorship and sign-offs must remain with the source. Niclan1 is the release maintainer and source host; upstream work retains its original attribution.
